# Sum-O-Primes

## Category
Cryptography

## Difficulty
Hard

## Description

We have so much faith in RSA that we decided to give you more information than you normally get.

You are given the product of two primes and their sum. Can you recover the private key and decrypt the message?

## Files

- `output.txt`

## Hints

1. I love squares :)
2. You know the sum and product of two numbers. What equation can give you the numbers themselves?

## Objective

Recover the two prime numbers, calculate the RSA private exponent, and decrypt the ciphertext to obtain the flag.

## Flag Format

`KiTS_CS_CTF{...}`
